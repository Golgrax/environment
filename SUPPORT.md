# Octovel Support Guide

The Octovel Support Guide outlines how to obtain help, report issues, and access community or commercial support for Octovel projects.  
It is designed to ensure an efficient and transparent process for troubleshooting, requesting features, and contributing to the ecosystem.

Octovel maintains clear support and contribution channels to provide a reliable experience for both individual developers and organizations.

---

## Getting Help

Before reporting an issue, consult the available resources to determine whether your question has already been addressed.  
Documentation and community platforms are continuously updated to assist users at every stage of development.

- **Documentation**: [https://docs.octovel.com](https://docs.octovel.com)  
- **Issue Tracker**: [https://github.com/octovel/.github/issues](https://github.com/octovel/.github/issues)
- **Discussion**: [https://github.com/octovel/.github/discussions](https://github.com/octovel/.github/discussions)  

If you encounter an error or unexpected behavior, please include detailed information such as your environment, logs, and reproduction steps when opening a report.

---

## Filing Issues

When submitting a bug or feature request, please ensure the report is precise and reproducible.  
Clearly describe the problem, the expected result, and any relevant technical context. Duplicate or vague reports may be closed for clarity.

For new ideas or improvements, explain the reasoning behind the proposal and, if possible, suggest potential implementation directions.

---

## Community & Communication

Community support is available through our official discussion platforms.  
Developers are encouraged to share feedback, ask questions, and collaborate openly.

- **GitHub Discussions**: [https://github.com/octovel/.github/discussions](https://github.com/octovel/.github/discussions)  
- **Stack Overflow**: [https://stackoverflow.com/questions/tagged/octovel](https://stackoverflow.com/questions/tagged/octovel)  
- **Discord**: [https://discord.gg/octovel](https://discord.gg/S83Emzj5bB)

For inquiries that require formal assistance or enterprise collaboration, please reach out via email at [support@octovel.com](mailto:support@octovel.com).

---

## Contributing and Security

Contributions are welcome and encouraged across all Octovel projects.  
Refer to the contribution guidelines in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) before submitting pull requests.

For security-related matters, consult [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) to report vulnerabilities responsibly.
